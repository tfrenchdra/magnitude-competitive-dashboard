What's working

The PR strategy is landing. The Fortune piece on OpenAI's balance sheet mentions Magnitude — that's the kind of contextual mention that compounds. TheCyberWire newsletter coverage on the $100M seed is the second tier-1 earned link. These are exactly the high-DR editorial links Sean's PR plan was designed to generate.

What needs attention

The spam backlink campaign is real, ongoing (new spam links arriving as recently as June 23), and pattern-matched: it's not random — it's a specific scraper-spammer template hitting multiple Magnitude-related domains (archerfaris.com, agentic.security, magnitude.ai). Disavow file is the next concrete action. Without it, the spam will keep accumulating and may eventually hit a threshold Google takes action on.

Structural gap between technical differentiation and commercial visibility

medium

Magnitude owns 'Autonomous TPRM Agents' but sits at 0.33% SoV with 2 mentions vs 50–70+ for Bitsight, ServiceNow, OneTrust. ChatGPT knows the story but doesn't see it often enough.

Recommendations:
  • Shift GTM from depth-only to depth-plus-reach.
  • Seed third-party content in 'continuous vendor monitoring,' 'regulated industries,' 'Fortune 500 VRM,' 'best TPRM tools' naming Magnitude alongside incumbents.

Business-value narrative is too AI-mechanics-focused

medium

Magnitude under-indexes on Audit-Ready Reporting, Integrated Evidence, Executive Dashboards. ChatGPT explains Magnitude as 'autonomous workforce' but rarely links to CFO/board KPIs.

Recommendations:
  • Reorient every technical capability to ladder to a business driver: audit cycles, remediations, critical-vendor coverage, manual hours, executive dashboards.
  • Develop canonical 'business outcome' stories present across site, analyst notes, customer interviews.

Own the AI-forward complex-supply-chain ICP

medium

Magnitude is seen as overkill for small portfolios but compelling for orgs with large vendor counts, complex Nth-party exposure, AI-heavy stacks.

Recommendations:
  • Double down on the mythos-era ICP: product, references, messaging, partnerships.
  • Build verticalized deep stories (fintech, global SaaS, critical infrastructure).
  • Get customers to speak as category exemplars.
